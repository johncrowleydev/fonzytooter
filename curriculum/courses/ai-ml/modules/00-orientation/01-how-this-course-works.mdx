Fonzytooter is self-paced. Modules have an intentional sequence and concepts may have prerequisites, but there is no weekly schedule to keep up with.

Use lessons to understand a concept, exercises to work with it, reviews to retain it, and projects to integrate multiple ideas into something less scripted.

The embedded tutor is available throughout the application. Use it to ask questions, explore tangents, debug misunderstandings, or request a quiz. For foundational coding exercises, use the tutor as a teacher rather than immediately outsourcing the implementation.
